How to label
  • Each card is one person as the tracker saw them at this moment: all camera crops it assigned to that person. The yellow box marks the detection.
  • Pick the team, then the jersey number. Use ? when you can tell the team but not the number. Refs: A/B if you can tell them apart, otherwise ?.
  • Not a player: spectator, bench, staff, or a box on nobody. Unclear: can't tell who it is.
  • If the crops on one card show different people, tap Crops are different people and label the odd ones.
  • The guess comes from the tracker and can be wrong; nothing counts until you tap. Tap a crop to enlarge it. The court map shows where each card's person is; tap a dot to jump to its card.
  • Labels save as you go. You can stop and come back any time, from any device.
